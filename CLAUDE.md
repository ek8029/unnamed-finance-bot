## Helm Outbound

- Helm Financial Corp. (me, Evan Kim, evan@helmterminal.dev) runs two things: Helm Terminal, the investment research product this site is about, and Helm Outbound, a managed outbound prospecting service plus small workflow automations for owner-led service businesses.
- Buyers: owner-led businesses that sell to other businesses, in any industry. The page stopped naming industries on 2026-10-13. The original focus was commercial cleaning, staffing and recruiting, security guard companies, landscaping and grounds, building trades, and independent insurance agencies. They are busy owners, not tech people. Copy must be plain, short and concrete.
- How outbound works: the client picks the kind of business, area and size; I research prospects from public records (permits, licenses, property sales, new registrations, company announcements, careers pages) and each prospect's own site; each gets a named contact with the source; emails go out in the client's name from a separate address on a domain set up only for this, with wording the client approves; up to two follow-ups; interested replies go to the client. No bought lists, and I do not sell leads.
- Offer: a free sample of ten researched prospects (reason, named contact with source, drafted email; nothing sent, no commitment). Then a 30-day pilot: a few hundred prospects, first email plus two follow-ups each; $1,000 to start, and the second $1,000 is due only if the pilot brings at least three interested replies. After that, monthly from $1,000 a month depending on volume.
- Workflow automation: small AI tools around one repetitive task (inquiry intake, follow-up tracking, quote and proposal drafts, weekly reporting). Everything is drafted for a person to review. Free 20-minute review of the task, then a fixed quote.
- Copy rules: plain American English; no hype, no exclamation marks, no dashes used as punctuation; no claims about clients, results or reply rates (there are none to cite yet); every sentence names its subject.
