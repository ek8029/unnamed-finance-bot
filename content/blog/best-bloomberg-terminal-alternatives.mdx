The best Bloomberg Terminal alternative depends on the work you want to finish. **Start with Koyfin for fundamentals, TradingView for charts, or Finviz for screening.** If your question is whether the evidence still supports a stock you own, start with **Helm's free analysis and one monitored investment thesis**.

Pick the tool that addresses a gap in your current process, then check whether its free plan covers that task.

**Disclosure:** I'm Evan Kim, Helm Terminal's founder. Helm is included here and offers paid subscriptions. This comparison is based on official product and plan information checked on September 12, 2026. Prices, data access and limits can change.

## Quick comparison: choose the task first

| Research financial statements and compare companies | **Koyfin** | Financial history, estimates and portfolio analytics vary by plan |
| Study price charts and build technical indicators | **TradingView** | Chart and alert limits; exchange data can cost extra |

## Helm Terminal: keep your investment thesis accountable

A stock can rise while the reason you bought it weakens. A headline can sound important while having little bearing on your original thesis. Helm is built around making that distinction easier to review.

Start with a stock you know. Read its [public analysis](/analyze), write down the reasons you would hold it, and review the evidence alongside those reasons over time. Add positions manually or connect supported accounts through Plaid for portfolio context. A brokerage connection is optional.

**Free includes one monitored thesis**, evidence history, portfolio visibility and concentration analysis, a general daily market brief, and a harvestable-loss headline figure from available portfolio data. Monitoring runs on a schedule; it does not promise an immediate alert or a new finding on every check.

**[Pro is $20/month or $149/year](/pricing).** It extends monitoring across your tracked theses and adds a personalized brief, earnings exposure, and detailed tax lots and wash-sale screening. The Free loss headline is not the full Pro tax workflow.

Helm fits investors who already have ideas and want a repeatable way to review them. It does not replace Bloomberg's professional data and execution environment or promise better returns.

## Koyfin: fundamentals, market dashboards and portfolio tools

Koyfin is worth considering when your work starts with financial statements, estimates, valuation comparisons and market dashboards. Its Free plan includes My Portfolios as well as limited financial history and screening.

Paid plans add deeper history and research access; advanced portfolio analytics and advisor integrations have their own plan boundaries. Check those boundaries against your needs before choosing a tier. [Koyfin features and pricing](https://www.koyfin.com/pricing/).

## TradingView: charts and technical workflows

TradingView brings charting, Pine Script, screeners, fundamentals and portfolio features together, with trading through supported brokers. The relevant distinction is how many charts, indicators and alerts your workflow needs.

Basic access is free. Some exchange data subscriptions cost extra even on a paid plan, so check the feed you need before comparing subscription prices. [TradingView plan and market-data details](https://www.tradingview.com/pricing/).

## Yahoo Finance: quick lookups and a familiar starting point

Yahoo Finance's free offering covers quotes, headlines, watchlists, charts and financial statements. It is a practical starting point for checking a company before opening a more specialized research tool.

Paid tiers add capabilities such as portfolio performance and risk analysis, research reports, valuations, deeper history and exports. Those features are not all included in free access. [Yahoo Finance's official plan guide](https://ca.help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN36623.html).

## Seeking Alpha: research arguments and ratings

Seeking Alpha is a fit for reading investment arguments and comparing them with ratings. Premium includes research articles, Quant Ratings and portfolio features such as broker linking and a Portfolio Health Check.

If you want to track your own reasons for owning a stock, compare the workflow for maintaining those reasons with the workflow for reading and rating someone else's research. [Seeking Alpha Premium features](https://help.seekingalpha.com/premium/seeking-alpha-premium-feature-list) and [subscription options](https://seekingalpha.com/subscriptions).

## Finviz: screening and visual market scans

Finviz is useful for filtering a stock universe and scanning sector heat maps. Free access is a starting point for generating a shortlist; Elite adds real-time stock data, expanded tools, alerts and exports.

Finviz also has portfolio features. The decision is whether the data timing and feature limits fit your process. [Finviz Free and Elite comparison](https://finviz.com/elite).

## OpenBB: configurable research, with two starting points

OpenBB belongs on a Bloomberg alternatives shortlist, but "free Python terminal" no longer describes its whole offering. **OpenBB Community is a free hosted edition.** The **Open Data Platform** is a separate open-source foundation for connecting public, licensed and proprietary data through interfaces including Python and a command line.

Commercial editions serve other deployment and team needs. Connected data can carry its own licensing fees and limits; included data access is limited. Choose this route when control over sources and research workflows matters to you. [OpenBB editions](https://openbb.co/pricing/) and [Open Data Platform documentation](https://docs.openbb.co/odp).

## Fiscal.ai, formerly FinChat: company operating detail

Fiscal.ai is worth a look for research that goes beyond headline financial ratios into company segments and operating KPIs. Its current official research documentation covers financial statements, ratios, segment data and KPI analysis.

The useful comparison is company-research depth: can you inspect the operating drivers behind your thesis? Check current consumer plan limits before subscribing. [Fiscal.ai research documentation](https://docs.fiscal.ai/docs/guides/mcp-skills) and [current plans](https://fiscal.ai/pricing/).

## Composer: strategy automation

Composer offers AI-assisted strategy creation, a visual editor, backtesting and automated execution. It addresses turning a defined strategy into a running process, a different purchase decision from choosing a market-data or research subscription.

Review its subscription and brokerage requirements before funding an account. Backtest results describe a historical simulation, not a guaranteed future result. [Composer's current product and pricing information](https://www.composer.trade/).

## What a retail tool stack does not replace

Bloomberg combines cross-asset data, news, research, analytics, collaboration and execution workflows. If your job depends on a particular feed, asset class or institutional network, validate that requirement directly. [Bloomberg Terminal's official product overview and demo request](https://professional.bloomberg.com/products/bloomberg-terminal/).

Get a current quote for the access you need, then compare it with the cost of the specific alternatives you would actually use. This guide does not assume a universal Terminal price or minimum contract term.
