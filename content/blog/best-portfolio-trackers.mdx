# Best Portfolio Trackers for Multiple Accounts in 2026

If you invest across multiple brokerage accounts — a 401(k) here, a Roth IRA there, a taxable account somewhere else — you know the pain of checking three different apps to understand your complete financial picture. The **best portfolio trackers** in 2026 solve this by aggregating all your accounts into a single view, showing your true asset allocation, and (in the best cases) proactively telling you what needs attention.

We evaluated seven portfolio management apps on their ability to handle multiple accounts, provide meaningful intelligence, and deliver real value beyond basic balance display.

## What Makes a Great Portfolio Tracker in 2026?

The bar has risen significantly. A modern portfolio tracker should deliver:

- **Automatic account linking**: Connect via Plaid or direct API — no manual entry of every trade
- **True aggregation**: Show combined allocation, performance, and risk across ALL accounts
- **Intelligent alerts**: Tell you when something needs attention without requiring daily manual review
- **Real-time data**: Live pricing during market hours, not yesterday's close
- **Multi-asset support**: Stocks, ETFs, bonds, options, crypto, and cash positions
- **Historical performance**: Accurate time-weighted returns including dividends and deposits/withdrawals

## 2. Empower (Personal Capital)

**Best for: Free net worth tracking with basic investment views**

Empower remains the most well-known free portfolio tracker. Its dashboard shows your complete net worth across investment accounts, bank accounts, and property. For more detail on Empower and when to consider switching, see our [Empower alternatives](/blog/best-personal-capital-alternatives) guide.

### Key Features

- Free account aggregation across all financial account types
- Net worth tracking with historical charts
- Asset allocation view (combined across accounts)
- Basic retirement planning calculator
- Fee analyzer to identify high-expense-ratio funds
- Investment checkup comparing your allocation to targets

### Limitations

- Persistent upselling toward advisory services (especially above $100K)
- No proactive intelligence or automated portfolio alerts
- Account syncing reliability has degraded for some institutions
- No real-time market data
- Dashboard innovation has stalled since the rebrand
- The free tool exists to sell a paid advisory product

### Pricing

Free (the product is the advisory service funnel).

### Verdict

Empower is a solid free tracker that shows you your numbers. It does not tell you what those numbers mean or what to do about them. The advisory upselling is the price you pay for "free."

## 3. Kubera

**Best for: Tracking complex wealth across alternative and international assets**

Kubera shines when your portfolio extends beyond standard US brokerage accounts. Real estate, crypto wallets, international banks, art, private equity, domains — Kubera tracks asset types that no other platform handles comprehensively.

### Key Features

- Broadest asset class support in any tracker (crypto, real estate, art, private equity, vehicles)
- Direct crypto wallet connections (including DeFi protocols)
- International bank and brokerage support
- Manual asset entry with custom valuations
- Beneficiary designation and estate planning documents
- Multi-currency net worth tracking
- Tickers, API feeds, and Zillow Zestimate integrations

### Limitations

- No free tier ($15/month minimum)
- No automated investment intelligence or proactive alerts
- Interface is functional but not modern
- No budgeting, spending, or cash flow features
- Limited performance analytics compared to dedicated investment trackers
- Small development team — feature updates are infrequent

### Pricing

Individual at $15/month, Family at $22/month.

### Verdict

If you own crypto wallets, international property, private investments, and collectibles alongside your standard brokerage accounts, Kubera is the only tracker that handles all of it. For straightforward US brokerage tracking with intelligence, other options deliver more value.

## 4. Stock Events

**Best for: Dividend investors tracking income across multiple accounts**

Stock Events has become the darling of the dividend investing community. It excels at visualizing your dividend income stream — showing exactly when payments arrive, projected annual income, and yield-on-cost metrics across all your holdings.

### Key Features

- Beautiful dividend calendar and income projections
- Automatic dividend detection and tracking
- Stock split, earnings, and ex-date event tracking
- Portfolio performance with dividend-inclusive returns
- Widget support for quick portfolio checks on mobile
- Multiple portfolio support (separate accounts)
- Global stock coverage

### Limitations

- Account linking is limited — many positions must be entered manually
- No automated intelligence beyond event notifications
- No combined allocation view across accounts (each portfolio is separate)
- Analysis is basic — focuses on events, not risk or strategy
- Free tier has meaningful restrictions

### Pricing

Free (limited), Premium at $8/month or $50/year.

### Verdict

Stock Events is the best dividend-tracking app available. But it is a dividend tracker that also does portfolio tracking, not a portfolio intelligence platform. If your primary concern is "when do I get paid?", it delivers perfectly.

## 5. Delta

**Best for: Crypto-heavy investors who also hold stocks**

Delta (by eToro) was originally built as a crypto portfolio tracker and expanded into stocks and ETFs. It remains the strongest option for investors whose portfolios span both traditional markets and cryptocurrency.

### Key Features

- Comprehensive crypto support (thousands of tokens, DeFi protocols, NFTs)
- Stock and ETF tracking alongside crypto
- Broker connections and manual entry
- Watchlists and price alerts
- Multi-portfolio management (different strategies or accounts)
- News aggregation by holding
- Clean mobile-first design

### Limitations

- Intelligence and analytics are basic — no automated insights
- Stock market features lag behind crypto features (its origin)
- No aggregated allocation view that meaningfully combines crypto + stocks
- Free tier is limited to 2 connections
- Performance calculation can be inconsistent with complex portfolios
- Owned by eToro — some concerns about data usage

### Pricing

Free (limited), Premium at $10/month.

### Verdict

If crypto is a significant portion of your portfolio, Delta handles the combination of traditional and digital assets better than most. For purely stock-focused portfolios, dedicated tools offer more depth.

## 7. Sharesight

**Best for: Tax reporting and performance attribution (especially for Australian/NZ investors)**

Sharesight is a portfolio tracker built around tax reporting accuracy. It automatically tracks dividends, calculates capital gains, and generates tax-ready reports. Originally built for Australian and New Zealand investors, it now serves global markets.

### Key Features

- Automatic dividend tracking and reinvestment handling
- Tax report generation (CGT, unrealized gains, income reports)
- Broker CSV import and some broker feeds
- Performance reporting with multiple time periods
- Multi-currency and multi-exchange support
- Benchmark comparison (custom benchmarks)
- Accurate annualized return calculations

### Limitations

- Account linking is limited — primarily CSV imports and select broker feeds
- No Plaid integration for US investors
- No automated intelligence or proactive alerts
- Free tier limited to 10 holdings
- Interface is functional but dated
- Not real-time — updates once daily for most data

### Pricing

Free (10 holdings), Investor at $10/month, Expert at $22/month.

### Verdict

Sharesight is exceptional for tax time. If your primary frustration is calculating capital gains across multiple accounts, it saves hours of spreadsheet work. As a daily portfolio monitoring tool, it is less compelling.

## How to Choose Your Portfolio Tracker

### Prioritize intelligence over display
The biggest gap in most portfolio trackers is not data — it is insight. Any tool can show you a balance. The question is: does it tell you what that balance MEANS for your risk, allocation, and upcoming events?

### Account linking is non-negotiable in 2026
If a tracker requires manual entry of every trade, it will become stale within weeks. Automatic Plaid-linked account syncing is the baseline requirement for managing multiple accounts.

### Consider your asset complexity
Standard US brokerage accounts? Helm, Empower, or Stock Events handle them well. Complex international + crypto + alternatives? Kubera is likely your only option.
