# Design Context — Helm Terminal

## Product
helmterminal.dev. An agentic financial intelligence terminal for individuals.
Reads every linked brokerage account and pushes what changed: exposure, tax
consequences, earnings, and whether the reason someone owns a position still
holds. Not a budgeting app. Not a portfolio tracker. Not an RIA.

## Target audience
Self-directed retail investors who outgrew Robinhood and Fidelity dashboards.
Multiple brokerages, $100k+ invested, they already read filings and follow the
tape. Sub-segments: engineers with RSUs, founders, finance professionals, HNW
individuals. They are not beginners and resent being talked to like one.

## Use cases
- One unified view across brokerages instead of five logins
- A daily brief that says what moved and why it matters to *their* book
- Tax-loss harvesting and Form 8949 support (the one deterministic dollar figure)
- Watching whether a thesis is weakening, with the source document quoted

## Brand personality / tone
Institutional, evidence-first, unadorned. The voice of a good analyst's memo:
declarative, specific, never breathless. Restraint is a brand asset. Every claim
is followed by its receipt. Confidence comes from citations and dates, not
adjectives.

**Never**: em dashes, Bloomberg comparison framing, advice language ("you
should buy"), hype adjectives, exclamation marks, emoji. Not an RIA.

## Visual system
- Background near-black `#060606` / `#0A0A0A`, never pure `#000`
- Gold `#E6B94D` is the single accent and it is scarce; `--color-gold-hi #FFD67A`
- Green `#4ADE80` positive, red `#F87171` negative. Semantic only, never decorative
- Fonts: `--font-sans` Geist, `--font-mono` Space Grotesk, `--font-display-serif`
  Instrument Serif (italic, used as an editorial accent on one word at a time)
- Tokens: `--color-bg-base|surface|elevated`, `--color-surface-tint(-faint)`, `--color-text-primary|secondary|muted`,
- `--font-newsreader` Newsreader is the editorial text serif, used only on /masthead and
  /this-week (their h1 is exempt from the sans headline rule). Four families total.
  `--color-border-subtle|base|strong`, `--color-gold*`, `--color-positive|negative`
- Chart/category ramp: `--chart-1..5` (account types, sector strips). Institution brand colours stay literal.
- Radii are small (5px on controls). Hairline rules over boxes. Tabular numerals.

## Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, React client components
- Must honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; the end state is always the readable one
- Mobile down to 393px; sidebar collapses below 1025px
- Any figure shown must be labelled demo data. Never a real user's holdings.

## Differentiation to express visually
Financial intelligence has always been pull. Helm makes it push. The proof is a
verbatim quote from a source document with a date next to it, on screen, before
the fold. Show the receipt rather than claiming accuracy.
